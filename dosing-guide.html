How to Use — Topical Serums
  1. Patch test first — a small amount on the inner forearm, wait 24 hours
  2. Cleanse and fully dry the skin or scalp
  3. Apply the serum first — moisturizer or oils go on after
  4. Keep away from eyes — rinse with water if contact occurs
  5. Recap tightly right after each use
Storage: Refrigerate at 36–46°F · Protect from light · Do not freeze · 12-month shelf life unopened
